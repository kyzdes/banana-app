## ✨ WHAT'S NEW

### 1. 🌟 PRESTIGE SYSTEM
**Ascend to greater power!**

- **Rebirth Mechanics**: Reset your clicks for permanent multipliers
- **Golden Bananas**: New premium currency earned through prestige
- **Prestige Perks**: 5 upgradeable perks for permanent bonuses
  - Golden Touch (+10% click power per level)
  - Automation Master (+20% auto-clicker efficiency)
  - Passive Income (+50% offline earnings)
  - Quest Master (+25% quest rewards)
  - Battle Ready (+15% PvP performance)
- **Prestige Levels**: Unlock at 1M, 10M, 100M clicks
- **Multiplier Stacking**: Each prestige increases your power permanently

### 2. 🛒 UPGRADES SHOP
**Deep progression with 9 unique upgrades!**

**Auto-Clickers:**
- Basic Auto-Clicker (1 CPS)
- Fast Auto-Clicker (10 CPS)
- Mega Auto-Clicker (100 CPS)

**Click Multipliers:**
- Double Tap (+100% click power)
- Power Click (+500% click power)
- God Finger (+2000% click power)

**Passive Income:**
- Banana Farm (5/sec)
- Banana Factory (50/sec)
- Banana Empire (500/sec)

Each upgrade has **100 levels** with exponential cost scaling!

**3 new quests every day!**

**Rewards:**
