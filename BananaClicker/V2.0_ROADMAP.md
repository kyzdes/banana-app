# 🍌 Banana Clicker V2.0 - Revolutionary Update

## Vision
Transform Banana Clicker from a simple clicker into a viral, social, competitive gaming phenomenon with deep progression systems, real-time multiplayer, and addictive engagement loops.

---

## 🎯 V2.0 Feature Categories

### 1. 🎮 PROGRESSION & ECONOMY
**Goal**: Create deep, rewarding progression that keeps players engaged for months

#### Prestige System
- **Rebirth Mechanics**: Reset clicks for permanent multipliers
- **Prestige Levels**: Unlock at 1M, 10M, 100M clicks
- **Golden Bananas**: Premium currency earned through prestige
- **Prestige Perks**: Permanent upgrades (faster auto-clickers, better rewards)
- **Prestige Leaderboard**: Compete on prestige level, not just clicks

#### Upgrades Shop
- **Auto-Clickers**: Passive click generation (1 CPS → 1000 CPS)
- **Click Multipliers**: 2x, 5x, 10x, 100x click power
- **Power-ups**: Temporary boosts (Frenzy Mode, Golden Hour, Double XP)
- **Banana Farms**: Generate clicks over time
- **Research Lab**: Unlock new upgrade paths
- **Cost Scaling**: Exponential growth for balanced economy

#### Currency System
- **Clicks**: Base currency
- **Golden Bananas**: Premium currency (prestige, achievements, IAP)
- **Gems**: Rare drops, daily rewards
- **Tournament Tokens**: Won in competitions

### 2. 🏆 COMPETITIVE FEATURES
**Goal**: Create thrilling PvP and competitive modes

#### Real-time PvP Battles
- **Quick Match**: 30-second clicking duels
- **Best of 3**: Tournament-style matches
- **Betting System**: Wager clicks on match outcomes
- **ELO Rating**: Skill-based matchmaking
- **Battle Arena Themes**: Different environments
- **Power-ups in Battle**: Strategic abilities

#### Guild/Clan System
- **Create/Join Guilds**: Up to 50 members
- **Guild Wars**: Weekly clan vs clan competitions
- **Guild Bonuses**: Member count boosts
- **Guild Chat**: In-app messaging
- **Guild Leaderboards**: Total guild clicks
- **Guild Raids**: Cooperative boss battles

#### Tournament Mode
- **Daily Tournaments**: 1-hour clicking competitions
- **Weekly Championships**: Bracket-style eliminations
- **Seasonal Grand Finals**: Top players compete
- **Spectator Mode**: Watch top players live
- **Tournament Shop**: Exclusive rewards
- **Casting System**: Share your tournament runs

### 3. 🎨 CUSTOMIZATION & COLLECTION
**Goal**: Give players creative expression and collection goals

#### Banana Skins (100+ skins)
- **Common**: Basic color variants (red, blue, green)
- **Rare**: Patterned (striped, polka dot, galaxy)

- **Mythic**: Ultra-rare (golden god, cosmic, diamond)
- **Seasonal**: Halloween, Christmas, Summer editions
- **Unlock Methods**: Achievements, shop, gacha system

#### Themes & Backgrounds
- **UI Themes**: Dark, Light, Neon, Retro, Minimal
- **Background Scenes**: Tropical, Space, Underwater, City
- **Sound Packs**: Classic, 8-bit, Epic, ASMR, Silent

- **Banners**: Background for profile

- **Emotes**: Use in battles and chat
- **Custom Fonts**: Personal style
